Blossom Hangman

How to Play

  • Try to guess the relaxation-themed word.
  • Click letters on the keyboard (or press them on your physical keyboard) to make a guess.
  • If a letter is correct, it will reveal its position.
  • If a letter is incorrect, a beautiful cherry blossom petal will fall from the tree branch.
  • You start with 6 petals. If all petals fall, the game ends.
  • Guess words successfully to earn points (+10 per correct letter, +50 bonus per word) and advance to the next word to accumulate a high score!
