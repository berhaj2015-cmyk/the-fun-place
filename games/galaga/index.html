Galaga Space Zen

How to Play

  • Use the Left & Right Arrow keys (or A & D) to move your spaceship.
  • Press the Spacebar to shoot stardust beams.
  • Pop the falling bubble targets before they reach the bottom of the screen.
  • If a bubble hits you or escapes past the bottom, you lose a life (💖).
  • Clearing all bubbles advances you to the next wave, which gets faster and more challenging!
